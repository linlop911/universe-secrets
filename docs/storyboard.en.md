# Six-chapter storyboard and final voice script

Tour length: 44 seconds per chapter. The rhythm is hook → setup → variable → reveal → comparison → close, with uneven beats at 0 / 6 / 14 / 24 / 33 / 39 seconds and a closing hold. The English voice line is shown as text with the tour; no recorded narration track is shipped.

Opening：Not even light can escape. So how can we see a black hole?

Core control：Change the spin and watch the ring and the inner disk。Source：[Explore NASA's black hole pages](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/black-holes/)。

| 6—14s | Setup | camera 12, 0.28, 0.8; param-spin=0.5, param-disk=1.4 | Light is how we find the dark | Yet around it, a ring of matter is glowing. | Accreting matter radiates, and some of that light bends toward us. The central shadow and the ring together form the structure we need to explain. |
| 14—24s | Variable | camera 8.2, 0.07, 1.65; param-spin=0.94, param-disk=1.65 | Light from the far side can still reach you | Drop toward the disk plane; light from the far side bends into view. | Move close to the disk plane. | 24—33s | Reveal | camera 8.2, 0.3, 2.2; param-spin=0.99, param-disk=1.65 | Change one variable yourself | Now change the spin yourself. | Drag the spin slider and compare the inner edge, brightness and theoretical geometry readouts. The image here is an empirical lensing demonstration. |
| 33—39s | Comparison | camera 10, 0.45, 2.7; param-spin=0.94, param-disk=1.3 | The ring is not the horizon | The ring is not the horizon. Key idea：This ring was reconstructed from EHT radio interferometry. It constrains the radiation and shadow around the black hole; the procedural disk on the left illustrates light bending, and the two images are not pixel-aligned.

Visual climax：The ring is not the horizon。Memory point：Not even light can escape. So how can we see a black hole?
